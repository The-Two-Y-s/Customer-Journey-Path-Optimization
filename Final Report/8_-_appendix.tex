\noindent\begin{sloppypar}The complete source code for the \textit{Customer Journey Path Optimization} framework (Python~3.12) is publicly available at:\end{sloppypar}

\smallskip
\noindent\centerline{\textbf{\url{https://github.com/The-Two-Y-s/Customer-Journey-Path-Optimization}}}

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Repository structure.}
The codebase is organised into four layers:

\begin{table}[h!]
\centering\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Module} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\texttt{src/dijkstra.py}            & Baseline \& probability-pruned Dijkstra (Algorithms 1--2) \\
\texttt{src/graph\_builder.py}       & Adjacency-list graph construction from transition data \\
\texttt{src/preprocessing.py}       & Clickstream CSV ingestion \& MLE probability computation \\
\texttt{src/critical\_tau.py}        & Adaptive fraction sweep for critical $\tau^*$ \\
\texttt{data/graph\_generator.py}    & ER \& layered synthetic generators (deterministic \texttt{md5} seeds) \\
\texttt{data/real\_data\_loader.py}   & RetailRocket \& RecSys~2015 YOOCHOOSE parsers \\
\texttt{run\_experiments.py}         & Synthetic experiment runner (2,160 configurations) \\
\texttt{run\_real\_experiments.py}    & Real-data experiment runner (840 configurations) \\
\texttt{analysis.ipynb}             & Plotting \& Wilcoxon signed-rank statistical tests \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent Both algorithms return structured results containing the optimal path, log-cost, path probability, and four performance counters (nodes explored, edges examined, edges relaxed, max priority-queue size). Installation instructions and reproduction commands are provided in the repository README.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Live interactive dashboard.}
An interactive visualisation of the experimental results is deployed at:

\smallskip
\centerline{\textbf{\url{https://the-two-y-customer-journey-path-optimization.vercel.app/}}}

\smallskip
\noindent Built with React~18 and Vite, auto-deployed on Vercel, the dashboard presents speedup vs.\ $\tau$ curves, optimality gap analysis, scalability plots, the critical~$\tau^*$ heatmap, and KPI summary cards derived from the experiment CSV files.
